# 01 — Vision & Pillars

## Vision statement

*Three Kingdoms: Mandate of Heaven* lets you live the fantasy of a warlord in
the most romanticized era of Chinese history. You inherit ROTK's defining
loop — grow a province, recruit legendary officers, out-scheme rivals, win
epic battles, and unify the realm — but rebuilt with a modern engine, modern
interface, and a living world that reacts and remembers.

We are **not** making *Total War: Three Kingdoms* (a battle-first RTS) nor
*Dynasty Warriors* (an action hack-and-slash). We are making the **grand
strategy / kingdom-management RPG** that the numbered ROTK series is, modernized.

## Design pillars

1. **The Officer is the atom.** Everything — armies, cities, plots, story — is
   expressed through named historical characters who have ambitions, loyalties,
   relationships, and mortality. The simulation is a drama of people.

2. **Strategy with a human face.** Numbers matter, but every decision is framed
   as a relationship or a story beat, not a spreadsheet cell. Modernized UX
   makes depth legible.

3. **The realm is alive.** Provinces, factions, and rivals act on their own
   agendas every turn. The world would keep turning without you.

4. **Romance over realism, history over fantasy.** We follow the *Sanguo Yanyi*
   (novel) tone — heroic, dramatic, larger-than-life — while keeping the real
   geography, factions, and timeline as the skeleton.

5. **Your depth, your pace.** The same game serves a 6-hour story-runner and a
   200-hour grand-campaign optimizer through difficulty and delegation systems.

## Target audience

- **Primary:** Strategy/4X and grand-strategy players (Paradox, Total War,
  Civilization, Crusader Kings audiences) and lapsed/current ROTK fans.
- **Secondary:** Fans of Three Kingdoms fiction, history, and the *Dynasty
  Warriors* universe wanting a deeper experience.
- **Tertiary:** Narrative-RPG players drawn in by the character drama and
  Story difficulty.

## Comparable / reference titles

| Title | What we take | What we change |
|-------|--------------|----------------|
| ROTK XI / XIV (Koei) | Officer system, grand campaign loop, scenarios | Modern UI, real-time-with-pause battles, living court |
| Crusader Kings III | Character lifecycle, schemes, relationships, traits | Setting, unification win-condition, named historical roster |
| Total War: Three Kingdoms | Battle spectacle, blended realism/romance modes | Battles are tactical RTwP, not the core; strategy layer is deeper |
| Civilization VI | Onboarding, advisor clarity, readable iconography | Single-era focused, character-driven |
| Field of Glory / Wargame UIs | Information density done cleanly | Approachability via progressive disclosure |

## Platforms

- **Primary:** PC (Windows) via Steam/Epic; designed mouse-first with full
  keyboard support.
- **Secondary:** Mac/Linux (same engine build).
- **Stretch:** Console (Xbox/PS) and high-end tablet with a controller/touch
  UI variant — the layered UI is designed to scale down.

## Success criteria (definition of "good")

- A new player reaches their first won battle and first recruited officer
  within 20 minutes without reading a manual.
- A veteran can disable advisors and micro-manage every officer and city.
- "Just one more turn" retention: median session > 45 minutes.
- The court/officer drama generates stories players want to share.
